> faq

What is a fractional CTPO?

A fractional CTPO is a part-time Chief Technology & Product Officer who joins your leadership team for a set share of their time, owning technology and product direction without the cost and commitment of a full-time executive hire.

What is the difference between working with Alexandre and with Abstract Extraordinary?

Fractional CTPO and advisory work is Alexandre Carvalho personally, as part of your leadership team. Abstract Extraordinary is his studio: a senior team that designs, builds and operates the AI system with you.

Does Alexandre work remotely?

Yes. He is based in Lisbon, Portugal and has led teams in Lisbon and London, working with companies in Europe and the US.
