> about me

I'm Alexandre Carvalho, a CTPO and AI product & engineering leader based in Lisbon, Portugal. I've spent 20+ years bridging technology and business: building teams, shipping products and scaling them.

Most recently I was Chief Product & Technology Officer at Metaphysic.ai, where I led ML research, ML engineering and product. We were the sole AI VFX provider on Robert Zemeckis' Here, running real-time de-aging on set. Read the full story.

Before that I built engineering at Streetbees through its $12M Series A, built Indie Campers' tech and product team from 0 to 30, took LinkedCare's health-record platform into the US, and founded my own startup. I started out leading the Ruby on Rails team that built Besttables, later acquired by TripAdvisor. I studied Computer Science engineering at Instituto Superior Técnico.

> what I do now

I run Abstract Extraordinary, a senior engineering studio for AI systems that have to work: in production, in public, in real time. We take them from first sketch to shipped product.

I also work directly with founders and leadership teams as an advisor or fractional CTPO on AI product strategy, building and scaling tech and product teams, and turning generative AI from demos into products.
